# Harbor Ledger — Business Relationship Manager

A proof-of-concept BRM (Business Relationship Manager) dashboard built to
demonstrate business banking product knowledge and client relationship
tracking for the Pensacola, FL / Baldwin County, AL market.

**Live demo:** https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/

## What it does
- Tracks simulated business banking relationships (contact info, deposits,
  revenue, relationship health score, held products)
- Reference catalog of core business banking product lines (deposit/cash
  management, lending, government contracting banking, trade finance, etc.)
- Follow-up/task tracking tied to each client

All business names are fictional. Financial figures are illustrative and
randomly generated for demonstration purposes only — no real client or
account data is used.
